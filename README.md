🚦 AI-Powered Urban Traffic Management System

An intelligent traffic optimization system designed to reduce urban congestion through Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning (MARL). The platform simulates real-world traffic conditions and enables multiple AI-controlled traffic signals to collaborate and make adaptive decisions that improve overall traffic flow.

Built around realistic traffic simulations, the system demonstrates how artificial intelligence can be applied to modern smart-city infrastructure to enhance mobility, reduce delays, and improve transportation efficiency.

📌 About The Project

Urban traffic congestion is one of the most significant challenges faced by modern cities. Traditional traffic signal systems often rely on fixed timing schedules that cannot adapt effectively to changing traffic patterns.

This project addresses that challenge by implementing a network of intelligent traffic signal agents capable of learning from traffic conditions and coordinating with neighboring intersections. Through continuous interaction with the simulation environment, the agents develop strategies that optimize traffic movement across the entire road network.

The system utilizes realistic traffic simulations to evaluate how AI-driven decision-making can outperform conventional traffic control approaches.

⚙️ System Workflow
Step 1 – Traffic Network Generation
Road networks and intersections are created
Traffic infrastructure is configured
Simulation environment is initialized
Step 2 – Traffic Simulation
Vehicles are introduced into the environment
Traffic conditions evolve dynamically
Agents observe traffic states continuously
Step 3 – Agent Learning
Traffic light agents analyze traffic conditions
Actions are selected based on learned policies
Rewards are generated from traffic performance
Step 4 – Cooperative Optimization
Agents adapt their behavior over time
Coordination emerges through reinforcement learning
Network-wide traffic efficiency improves
Step 5 – Performance Evaluation
Traffic metrics are collected
Results are analyzed and visualized
AI-controlled performance is assessed
